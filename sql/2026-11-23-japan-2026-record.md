## What the evidence was
- **Flight.** United's emails (confirmation FWDDHP): UA 32 left LAX Sat Feb 28 11:05am and landed at
  **Narita** Sun Mar 1 3:55pm. koji had Mar 1 → Mar 2 into Haneda. Hotels.com shows the Takadanobaba
  stay from Sun Mar 1. So `date_start` is now 2026-02-28, the trip has 37 days, and a new day
  (Mon Mar 2, Kamakura) sits at sort_order 3; every later day moved up one.
- **Photos.** All 963 camera frames in `~/Pictures/2026/`. The camera clock was on US Central
  (EXIF offset -06:00), so Japan time = camera + 15 h and the folder names run a day behind for
  anything shot before 3pm. Checked against the Sapporo TV Tower clock in frame. Places below are
  ones a sign or landmark in the frame confirms.
- **Nathan (chat, 2026-11-23).** Lake Toya didn't happen; Hiroshima and Nagasaki did; the three
  shinkansen legs used Tokyo Station (train not remembered).
